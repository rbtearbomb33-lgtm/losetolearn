# V4.0 — Daily planner & growth

## Daily todos
- Todos are stored locally and date-stamped.
- Incomplete items remain attached to their original date and are not automatically carried into a new day.
- Important items support high / medium / low weight and are sorted by weight; ordinary items are always low weight.
- Items can be added, edited, checked off, and deleted.

## Growth
- Every 10 minutes of credited focus time grants 1 EXP (based on lifetime credited focus time).
- The first time daily credited focus reaches 30 minutes, grant 2 EXP.
- Reaching a 3 / 7 / 14 / 30-day streak of days with at least 30 credited focus minutes grants 3 / 7 / 14 / 30 EXP once for that streak run.
- A broken streak resets the current streak count, not previously earned EXP or the historical 7-day record.
- Levels are visual-only: level number, progress bar, and subtle styling. No store, currency, or items.
- Paused time is not credited. Manually stopped sessions credit only complete minutes, as before.

## Compatibility
- Existing V3.9 local data is migrated in place. Existing session history is used to estimate lifetime focus time and historical qualifying study days where possible.
- No cloud sync or account is used.
